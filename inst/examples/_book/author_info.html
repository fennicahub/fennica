2  Author information: names, dates, gender, and profession

Author-level information in Fennica, including names, authority identifiers, dates, gender, profession, language, and other biographical metadata for the complete dataset and the 1809–1917 subset.

3 Author database

3.1 Description

The author database provides an author-level view of people associated with publications in Fennica. It combines information recorded in the MARC 100 field for main authors and the MARC 700 field for added authors.

Authors are identified primarily through Asteri authority identifiers when these are available. Asteri identifiers make it possible to connect different name forms referring to the same person and to link bibliographic records with information from the Kanto national authority database.

Authors without an Asteri identifier are retained using harmonized author names. This makes it possible to include authors who occur in Fennica but do not have an authority identifier.

The database therefore combines information from two main sources:

  • Fennica bibliographic records, including author names and publication information; and
  • Kanto authority data, providing additional biographical and authority information.
Datasets presented on this page

Two versions of the author database are presented below.

Complete Fennica author database contains authors associated with records across the complete harmonized Fennica dataset.

1809–1917 author database contains authors associated with publications included in the historical 1809–1917 subset.

The 1809–1917 database is therefore a subset of the complete author database.

3.2 Author identification

Information used to identify authors includes:

  • harmonized author names;
  • alternative and variant names;
  • Asteri authority identifiers;
  • names extracted from MARC 100 and MARC 700 fields; and
  • links between authors and Fennica bibliographic records.

A single author may appear in multiple Fennica records and may be represented by several name forms in the original bibliographic metadata.

3.3 Biographical information

Where corresponding authority information is available, author records are enriched with variables describing the person, including:

  • birth and death dates;
  • calculated age;
  • gender;
  • profession;
  • title;
  • field of activity;
  • language;
  • country; and
  • associated places.

The availability of these variables differs because not every author has an Asteri authority identifier and not every Kanto authority record contains the same amount of biographical information.

3.4 Publication activity

The database also contains variables describing an author’s occurrence in Fennica. Publication activity is calculated from the publication years of records associated with each author and includes measures such as:

  • number of associated Fennica records;
  • first observed publication year;
  • last observed publication year;
  • mean publication year; and
  • observed publication activity span.
Interpretation

Publication activity refers to an author’s representation in Fennica. It should not necessarily be interpreted as the author’s complete publication history.

Similarly, missing biographical information does not mean that the information is historically unknown. It means that the information could not be obtained from the bibliographic and authority metadata used in this workflow.

The percentages above describe metadata availability, not the proportion of authors possessing a particular characteristic.

4.3 Profession

The profession variable originates from authority information associated with the author. An author may have more than one profession recorded.

4.4 Titles

Titles describe titles or designations associated with persons in the authority data.

4.5 Language

Language information describes languages associated with persons in the authority data. It should therefore not be interpreted directly as the publication language of the corresponding Fennica records.

4.6 Field of activity

Field of activity provides a broader description of the professional, intellectual, artistic, or other fields associated with a person in the authority data.

4.7 Country

Country information is derived from authority metadata and describes countries associated with individual authors.

5 Authors in publications from 1809–1917

This section focuses on authors associated with Fennica publications included in the 1809–1917 historical subset.

Restricting the database to this period provides a more relevant description of authors represented in the nineteenth-century and early twentieth-century material used in the historical analyses.

What does 1809–1917 refer to?

The period refers to the publication years of the Fennica records, not to the birth years, death years, or active years of the authors.

An author can therefore appear in this subset even if their lifetime extends considerably before or after 1809–1917.
